## Scope of the claim

The whole walk was run by `Claude Opus 5 (1M context)` at effort `medium`, on awawa 2.7.0: what a step cost, what it
left unasked and what it concluded from a probe are measured under that agent and that effort alone, and another
model, or the same one at another effort, may pay a defect differently or not hit it at all. The eighteen defects are
read from the starter's text. Defects 1 to 4 are observed on one step boundary of one walk,
S1 to S2; defects 5 and 6 inside S3 of the same walk; defects 7 to 9 inside S4 of the same walk, on awawa 2.7.0;
defects 11 to 13 inside S5, on the same version; defects 14 and 15 on the S5 to S6 boundary, the second one
measured by the review the owner asked for there; defects 16 to 18 inside S6, on the same version. Nothing here is
measured over the eight steps: S7 and S8 had not run when this report was last written. Defects 8 and 9 are P10's, a principle S4 invokes; S5 applied their two
remediations — the whole area copied, a conforming and a non-conforming entity per conditional declaration — and the
three findings of defect 12 come from the non-conforming half alone, which is what the remediation was for. Defect 10 is of another kind:
it was not hit by this walk, which is on an implemented project and is served correctly by Flow S; it is read from
the coverage of the two flows, and its cost would be paid by another project, not by this one.
